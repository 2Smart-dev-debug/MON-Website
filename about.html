THE NIGHTMARE BEHIND THE NAME

About Smart Taggers

There is nowhere to hide.

Smart Taggers is a cutting-edge virtual reality horror game designed to deliver a spine-chilling experience that immerses players in a world of relentless terror. Created by Smart Studios, Smart Taggers takes you deep into an abandoned research facility where once docile chimpanzees have been turned into deadly, intelligent predators. Every corner you turn is filled with danger, and there’s nowhere to hide.

At the core of Smart Taggers is the atmosphere. Every creak of the floor, every flicker of the lights, and every distant growl from the mutated chimps creates an oppressive tension that keeps you on edge from the moment you put on the VR headset. The game’s terrifying world is more than just a setting—it’s a character in itself, one that you must navigate with nothing but your wits and reflexes.

The Story

In a forgotten research facility, experiments have gone horribly wrong. Once considered groundbreaking studies on animal intelligence, the facility now stands abandoned, its halls filled with nothing but the echoes of past horrors. The chimpanzees, once used as subjects in these cruel experiments, have mutated into intelligent, savage predators. You, as an unarmed and vulnerable survivor, must navigate the terrifying labyrinth of decaying rooms and hallways, all while being hunted by the relentless chimps who will stop at nothing to track you down.

Our Vision

At 2.Smart Studios, our goal is simple: to create unforgettable experiences that push the boundaries of what horror can be in virtual reality. Smart Taggers is not just about jump scares—it’s about immersing players in a world where fear feels real. We aim to make your heart race, your palms sweat, and your every decision a matter of life and death.

We believe that VR is the future of horror, and Smart Taggers is a testament to that belief. With every corner you turn, every sound you hear, and every shadow you glimpse, we’ve worked tirelessly to create an experience that will keep you coming back for more... as long as you can survive.

Meet the Team

Smart Taggers was created by a passionate trio of horror enthusiasts and game designers. Each member of the team brought their unique skills to the project to ensure the most terrifying and immersive experience possible. We’re dedicated to bringing our players the best in horror, and Smart Taggers is just the beginning.
